## Prototyping Architecture

Are the responsibilities of the major areas well defined and appropriate?
Are the collaborations between major components well defined?
Is coupling minimized?
Can you identify potential sources of duplications?
Are interfaces definitions and constraints acceptable?
Does every module have an ccess when it needs?
